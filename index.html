GigVault

The Problem

When disaster strikes New York City, gig workers are among the most financially vulnerable. Without employer-backed safety nets, no paid leave, no insurance continuity, no guaranteed income, they face immediate economic crisis on top of the disaster itself.

GigVault addresses this gap: a platform providing emergency fund access, income documentation, and financial recovery tools built specifically for the gig economy.

Reflection

The insight didn't come from any single interview…

It came from noticing what connected them. Each team member brought back different stories, but I kept seeing the same structural absence: no employer, no safety net, no record. Naming that pattern reframed our entire project. Designing the brand made this concrete. The logo isn't decorative. The "G" is the gig worker, and the slash is a shield. That metaphor forced me to ask: what does protection actually look like when you have no institutional backing? That question shaped every feature decision that followed. What I'll carry forward: human-centered design isn't just about listening to users. It's about holding multiple voices in tension until a shared truth emerges.
