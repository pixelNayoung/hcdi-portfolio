The Problem

When disaster strikes New York City, gig workers are among the most financially vulnerable. Without employer-backed safety nets, no paid leave, no insurance continuity, no guaranteed income, they face immediate economic crisis on top of the disaster itself.

GigVault addresses this gap: a platform providing emergency fund access, income documentation, and financial recovery tools built specifically for the gig economy.

My Contributions

Target Audience Discovery

Identified gig workers as a common thread across multiple team members' independent research. Proposed reframing the entire project around this underserved user group.

User Interviews

Conducted primary interviews with gig workers in NYC to understand their lived financial vulnerabilities during disasters.

Brand & Logo Design

Designed the GigVault visual identity and logo, establishing the project's brand direction from the ground up.

Design Strategy

Shaped the product concept through the human-centered design process, from empathy research through prototyping and evaluation.
